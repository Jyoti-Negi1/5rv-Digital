5. Data Controller & Data Processor Responsibilities

5RV Digital acts primarily as a Data Controller when collecting personal information directly through our website, contact forms, quotations, newsletters, and business communications.

However, while providing digital marketing and technical services, we may also act as a Data Processor on behalf of our clients.

This includes situations where clients authorise us to access:

  • Website hosting accounts
  • Website content management systems
  • Google Analytics (GA4)
  • Google Tag Manager
  • Google Search Console
  • CRM platforms
  • Email marketing platforms
  • Advertising accounts
  • Customer databases
  • Other business systems containing personal data

When acting as a Data Processor:

  • We process personal data only on the documented instructions of our client.
  • We maintain strict confidentiality over all client information.
  • Appropriate technical and organisational security measures are implemented to protect personal data.
  • Client information is never used for our own marketing purposes.
  • Access is restricted only to authorised personnel who require it to deliver contracted services.
