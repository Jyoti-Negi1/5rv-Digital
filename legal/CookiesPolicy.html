UK GDPR & Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) Compliant · Last Updated: July 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website.

They help websites function correctly, improve security, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors interact with online content.

At 5RV Digital, we use cookies to provide a secure browsing experience, improve website functionality, analyse website performance, and enhance the services we offer. Some cookies are essential for the operation of our website, while others help us understand visitor behaviour or measure the effectiveness of our marketing activities.

Where required by UK law, non-essential cookies are only placed on your device after you have provided your consent.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation and security of our website. Without them, certain features and services may not function correctly.

Strictly necessary cookies help us to:

  • Maintain secure browsing sessions
  • Remember your cookie preferences
  • Protect website forms against spam and malicious activity
  • Enable core website functionality
  • Support website administration and security

These cookies cannot be disabled through our Cookie Preference Centre.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

They may collect information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Visitor journeys throughout the website
  • Device and browser information
  • General website performance
  • Traffic sources

This information is aggregated and anonymous wherever possible and helps us continually improve our website.

Analytics cookies are only activated after you provide your consent.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies remember preferences you have previously selected to improve your browsing experience.

Examples include:

  • Language preferences
  • Accessibility settings
  • Saved form information
  • Website interface preferences

These cookies improve usability but are not essential for the website to operate.

Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies help us measure advertising performance and improve the relevance of our marketing campaigns.

Where enabled, these cookies may:

  • Measure campaign effectiveness
  • Track website conversions
  • Support remarketing activities
  • Improve advertising performance

Marketing cookies are only placed after you have provided your explicit consent.

5. Cookie Consent & Managing Preferences

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with our Cookie Consent Banner.

You may choose to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject all non-essential cookies
  • Manage your cookie preferences
  • Change your consent at any time

Manage Your Preferences

In line with UK GDPR and PECR requirements, all non-essential cookies are disabled by default. We do not use pre-ticked consent boxes.

You can update or withdraw your consent at any time using the Cookie Preference Centre available on our website.

6. Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

Most modern web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings.

You can usually:

  • View stored cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Block all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Clear browsing data

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and performance of some parts of our website.

7. Data Retention

We retain cookie-related information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Cookie Policy and to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where cookies collect information relating to business enquiries or website interactions:

  • Business lead information may be retained for up to 3 years following the last meaningful interaction.
  • Client contractual records may be retained for 6–7 years to comply with UK tax, accounting, and contractual obligations.
  • Analytics and performance data are retained according to the retention periods configured within the relevant third-party platforms.

Once retention periods expire, data is securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.
