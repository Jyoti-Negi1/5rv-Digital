How We Approach Web Development that Scales

As a web development agency with over 27 years of experience, we've seen how user behavior online has changed. Simply throwing up a generic template no longer works. To create a website that works for your company, we follow a simple six-step approach:

  1. 01

    Investigating Your Business and Current Site

    A website should not be planned on assumptions. Understanding your business comes first. We review your current website, visitor data, services, and competitors before starting any design or development work.

  2. 02

    Platform Selection

    The platform is chosen after we understand what the website needs to do. Most business websites are built on WordPress, while larger or more complex projects may require a different solution.

  3. 03

    Intent-Based UI/UX

    People visit websites to find information, compare services, or make contact. The page structure and navigation should help them do this without unnecessary searching.

  4. 04

    Testing & Quality Assurance

    A website should perform on a staging environment before it goes live. Every page, form, link, and feature we establish goes through deep testing and quality assurance.

  5. 05

    Cross-Device Performance

    People use websites on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. A website should work properly on all screen sizes, not just on one device.

  6. 06

    Contact Points

    Putting contact points at the end of the website is not always the best approach. People often decide to make contact while reading a page. We plan button placements instead of placing them in the same position on every page.
