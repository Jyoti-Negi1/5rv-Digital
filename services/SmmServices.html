How We Approach Social Media Marketing

Having a website is a good start, but most buyers check your social media pages before they contact you. If your pages are empty or out of date, you lose business. Here is how we build an active social media presence that brings in real customers:

  1. 01

    Checking your current digital presence

    We inspect your website and social accounts to see how buyers find you. We study your local competitors and identify opportunities you're missing, giving us a clear baseline to build your growth plan.

  2. 02

    Setting out your strategy and calendar

    We do not post randomly. We first create a simple 30-day content calendar that shows what we will post, where we will publish, and how each post will help bring in new enquiries.

  3. 03

    Profile setup and branding

    We update your logos, cover images, contact details, and bio text across all channels. We make sure your profiles look fully professional so visitors immediately trust your business.

  4. 04

    Creating user-focused content

    We produce clean graphics and real project photos showing your work. We explain your services and directly answer the questions local customers ask before buying.

  5. 05

    Running focused local advertising

    We run targeted paid ad campaigns to reach people in your specific area who need your services right now. These ads send qualified local buyers straight to your website or inbox.

  6. 06

    Tracking performance and sending clear reports

    We review your account numbers weekly and send you a simple monthly report. You will see exactly how many people saw your business and how many lead enquiries came through.
