How We Approach SEO That Succeeds

Optimising to rank higher no longer means keyword stuffing or buying backlinks. With AI overviews and evolving search algorithms, being in the top search results has become more competitive. 5RV Digital solves these challenges in the following ways:

  1. 01

    Finding out where you stand

    We first set up an initial meeting with your team to learn about your goals, site status, and relevant competitors. Under this step, we research the exact search terms your prospective buyers use online.

  2. 02

    Setting up your action plan

    Once we understand your goals and current performance, we create a strategic plan for your business. We move with a 30-day plan for urgent site fixes and a 90-day plan for steady growth.

  3. 03

    Fixing the technical foundations

    Once the plan is set, we start by fixing technical errors. First, we resolve backend issues that block Google from indexing your site. Afterwards, we improve page load speeds and fix broken links.

  4. 04

    Writing for your actual buyers

    Next, we optimise your web pages and blog posts with high-performing, intent-matched keywords. We update the content with clear, simple text that directly answers customer questions.

  5. 05

    Building your site's authority

    Search engines rank websites higher when other established platforms trust them. We review your backlink profile and secure links from relevant, high-quality websites in your industry.

  6. 06

    Measuring, reporting & improving

    From day one, we set up clear tracking systems to monitor your site's performance. Through short weekly updates and a monthly report, we also keep you informed.
