How we turn business into a brand

A logo is not the only branding businesses need. Businesses become brands when they look credible even before a customer speaks to them. If your branding looks old or inconsistent, you lose business. At 5RV Digital, we follow a strategic process:

  1. 01

    Analysing your local competitors

    We start by checking other companies that offer the same services in your area. Seeing how they present themselves shows us where their presentation looks poor, so we can make your business look like the better option.

  2. 02

    Writing clear business descriptions

    People want to know what you do without having to read complicated sales talk. We write plain text for your business that explains your services clearly on your estimates, website, and paper materials.

  3. 03

    Designing your visuals

    Your business needs a neat, matching look across everything you use. We build custom logos, pick easy-to-read fonts, and choose clear colours that look sharp on both physical signs and mobile screens.

  4. 04

    Setting simple rules for your team

    Printers and staff often mess up logos or use the wrong colours if nobody gives them instructions. We hand you a simple one-page guide that shows everyone exactly how your logo and colours should be used.

  5. 05

    Making your new look live

    A new logo does nothing if it stays saved on a computer. We apply your updated design directly onto your team shirts, job site banners, letterheads, and social media pages.

  6. 06

    Helping you charge fair rates

    When a company looks organized and professional, buyers expect to pay normal rates instead of asking for cheap discounts. A clean look gives customers confidence in your work before you even start the job.
